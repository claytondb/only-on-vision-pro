What counts as “only on Vision Pro”. Every app here has a US App Store listing that runs on Apple Vision Pro and nothing else. Apps that are also sold for iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV are left out, including iPhone and iPad apps that happen to run on Vision Pro.

How the list stays current. Every day an automated job reads Apple’s Vision Pro top charts (the US plus 12 other countries in rotation), the Vision Pro App Store’s own pages and every app from each Vision Pro developer, then checks each app against Apple’s catalog and its App Store page for the devices it supports. New Vision Pro–only apps are added and removed ones drop off. “Popular now” follows Apple’s US Vision Pro top charts, then the charts in 12 other countries; apps that aren’t charting anywhere come last, most recently updated first. Star ratings appear where the App Store shows them in its Vision Pro charts.
